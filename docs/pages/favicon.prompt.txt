Initial generation

Use case: logo-brand. Design a distinctive, art-directed square emblem for the Learning Notes / 学习笔记 website. The emblem should feel like a small engraved ex libris stamp or a piece of polished bookbinding hardware, refined and memorable. It must read at both 16 px favicon size and 42 px masthead size. Original single continuous form, a note page becoming a fountain pen nib, with real visual character.

Composition: one tall, cream-ivory sheet with a slightly irregular, beautifully tapered paper silhouette, rising from lower left to upper right as if a page is turned mid-writing. On the left half put exactly two dark forest-green ink strokes that suggest handwritten notes, one short soft arcing stroke over one slightly longer stroke. Make them bold and calligraphic, with clear taper and an intentional dry-brush silhouette but no tiny speckles. At the upper right, the broad sweeping curve of the page turns back over itself and joins a single warm satin-gold fountain pen nib pointing down and right. The ivory page edge and gold nib should connect as one clever sculptural silhouette, with just one dark engraved slit and one small circular breather hole to make the nib recognizable. No separate freestanding pen, no pencil, no generic rectangular document or triangular dog-ear. Make the page sweep and tapered nib give the silhouette rhythm, depth and a distinct diagonal energy. The icon must feel like the act of taking notes.

Place this single emblem centered on a deep, solid forest-green rounded-square tile, approximately #286249, with a quiet wide green margin around the ivory and gold form. Warm ivory and brushed champagne-gold only, with a restrained highlight on the curled page edge. High contrast, bold shapes, subtle relief limited to the page fold and gold nib, no fine decorative filigree, mockup, photographic background, lettering, type, numbers, shadows outside the tile, or watermark. A narrow transparent margin and genuinely transparent area only outside the four clean rounded corners of the tile. The entire green tile interior must be solid and opaque, including its upper-right region. The tile should fill about 94% of the square canvas. Render a clean finished website mark, one icon only.
